The thesis

From Exponential Organizations to ExO 3.0.

In 2014, Salim Ismail published Exponential Organizations, the book that named the operating pattern behind the fastest-growing companies of the last decade. Companies that followed its principles outperformed those that didn’t by 40X. A decade later, AI has changed the operating logic of the firm itself. Coordination cost — the reason firms exist in the first place — is collapsing. Workflows can now improve themselves at machine speed. Hierarchy is no longer the primary way work gets done.

We call that inflection point the Organizational Singularity. The new architecture that replaces hierarchy is ExO 3.0. Its operating core is the Intelligence Stack — six continuous cognitive layers wrapped by a live governance plane called GOVERN / ASSURE. We back companies that build around it from day one, sharing our latest learning and ideas before they become public (the way the Frontier AI Labs use the latest models internally before releasing them more broadly).

The operating model

Systematized Venture Capital™.

We have built Organizational Singularity Fund I as a venture platform, not a discretionary partnership. Two human partners and two AI venture partners work together on every stage of the investment cycle — sourcing, qualitative diligence, quantitative diligence, portfolio support, and learning loops back into the fund's own architecture. The AI partners are trained on the ExO and Organizational Singularity methodologies, venture capital best practices, and thousands of historical deals. This is not AI for show. It is core to how the fund operates.

The principles behind Systematized Venture Capital™ come from James’ 35 years in coaching, training and development (including being certified in 14 coaching systems) and 20+ overlapping years of experience running venture capital funds where he observed the companies with the highest valuation increases followed systems. To understand the observation better he went back to university for a late-life master’s degree at INSEAD and a doctorate at The Tavistock. At both, his research focused on applying Systems Psychodynamics to Venture Capital. The result is Systematized Venture Capital™ and the chosen system — after evaluating dozens — is Exponential Organizations.

First investment

Our first portfolio company: The 99 Kids.

In Q1 2026, the fund made its first investment in The 99 Kids, a values-driven children's media company built around the belief that every child deserves access to stories that reflect their culture, language, and inner courage. The company is using AI to produce personalized, age-appropriate content at scale.

From our side, it is the pilot environment for applying the Organizational Singularity playbook inside a venture-backed company: standing up the Intelligence Stack, designing the agent and data specifications, and running GOVERN / ASSURE in production. The lessons will feed directly into fund investments and inform our, and OpenExO, pilot cohorts.
