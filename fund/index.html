What we believe

What we believe.

Four convictions sit underneath everything we do.

  1. 01
    Coordination cost is collapsing.

    The firm persists as an accountability shell. The hierarchy inside it no longer is the primary way work gets done.

  2. 02
    Architecture is the new alpha.

    The companies that win this decade will not just use AI. They will rebuild their operating system around it. The Intelligence Stack — PURPOSE, SENSE, INTERPRET, DECIDE, ORCHESTRATE, LEARN — is that operating system.

  3. 03
    Governance is the moat.

    GOVERN / ASSURE is not compliance. It is a live control plane that makes the Stack safe enough to delegate real authority to. The firms that build it from day one will be the firms that other firms agree to transact with.

  4. 04
    The leadership team is coachable.

    Open to receiving active, Eyes In, Hands In™ support when and where helpful. Capital alone does not move the ten characteristics. Founders willing to work the system do.

Portfolio support

How we work with portfolio companies.

This is a done WITH you model, not done-for-you and not hands-off. We apply the same Organizational Singularity framework to every portfolio company — the Backcasting Canvas, ExO 3.0, the Intelligence Stack, REWRITE, and GOVERN / ASSURE — alongside the founding team. Salim Ismail's book The Organizational Singularity is the full thesis, continuously updated. Organizational Singularity Fund I is the venture investment vehicle built to exploit it.

  1. 01
    Backcast first.

    Before the first agent ships, we run the Backcasting Canvas: define the destination architecture, then work back. The roadmap follows the destination, not the other way round.

  2. 02
    Stand up the Minimal Viable Intelligence Stack.

    One event bus, basic agent registry, central logging, one agent per class. One week of work, decades of compounding.

  3. 03
    Design the Edge Twin (if it applies).

    For companies over 50 people, REWRITE runs at the edge — a small, board-mandated unit that rebuilds workflows from scratch and migrates them in.

  4. 04
    Operationalize GOVERN/ASSURE on day one.

    Trusted Evals, Searchable Logs, Granular Rollback, Human Review Queue. Score yourself 1 to 5 on each. We do not deploy new agent classes below a 3.

  5. 05
    Compound the playbook.

    Every learning from every portfolio company is captured, codified, and offered to the next one.

First investment

Our first portfolio company: The 99 Kids.

In Q1 2026, the fund made its first investment in The 99 Kids, a values-driven children's media company built around the belief that every child deserves access to stories that reflect their culture, language, and inner courage. The company is using proprietary AI tools to produce personalized, age-appropriate, multi-platform content at scale.

From our side, it is the pilot environment for applying the Organizational Singularity playbook inside a venture-backed company: standing up the Intelligence Stack, designing the agent and data specifications, and running GOVERN / ASSURE in production. The lessons will feed directly into fund investments and inform our, and OpenExO, pilot cohorts.
