What OpenExO is

A global ecosystem behind the methodology.

OpenExO is the global ecosystem behind the Exponential Organizations and Organizational Singularity methodologies. It is where coaches, consultants, operators, and subject matter experts meet the practitioners who are trying to apply the architecture inside real organizations. The fund is one application of the methodology. OpenExO is how the methodology reaches everyone else.

Backed by 50,000+ practitioners already exploring Exponential Organizations and the Organizational Singularity through OpenExO.

How the fund and OpenExO relate

The fund invests. OpenExO convenes.

The relationship is simple: the fund invests, OpenExO convenes. Founders building Intelligence-Stack-native companies that fit the venture thesis come to Exponential Venture Capital. Mid-market operators, family businesses, governments, non-profits, and the next wave of founders come to OpenExO. The two organizations share methodology, share learning, and share a commitment to making the Organizational Singularity a usable architecture, not a slogan.
